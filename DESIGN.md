## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined synthesis of Neubrutalism and modern modular bento-box structuring, tailored for the personal portfolio of a Senior Graphic Designer & Content Creator. The brand personality balances uncompromising creative authority with tactile playfulness, immediate clarity, and editorial rigor.

The visual style departs from standard raw brutalism by softening structural hard edges into generous, comfortable radiuses (16–24px) while retaining deliberate high-contrast geometry, hyper-tactile press states, offset shadow planes, and dense visual modularity. It rejects flimsy transparency and muddy atmospheric blurs in favor of grounded, physical slabs of color and razor-sharp line weights that communicate graphic design expertise and intentional craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette establishes structural equilibrium through high-contrast anchors:

- **Neutral Canvas (`#F4F4F5`):** An engineered industrial off-white functioning as the macro canvas, preventing display glare while giving crisp definition to floating white modules.
- **Primary Ink (`#0F172A`):** Deep, impenetrable slate black reserved for primary typography, structural silhouettes, hard-edged shadow planes, and high-status badges.
- **Secondary Energy (`#F59E0B`):** Warm marigold yellow employed sparingly to flag focal modules, hero highlights, direct calls to action, and dynamic portfolio tags.
- **Tertiary Border & Divider (`#E4E4E7`):** Architectural boundary gray utilized for structural card contours, ensuring clean module containment without visual clutter.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure stark white utilized for primary bento cells to pop against the off-white canvas.

### State & Contrast Applications
- **Interactive Hover:** Elements anchored in `#F59E0B` invert with sharp slate strokes or snap downward along their hard drop-shadow trajectory.
- **Micro-Accents:** Subtle slate-on-marigold pairings communicate urgency and editorial punch within stat badges, project categories, and availability indicators.

## Typography

The typography couples the bold geometric tension of **Space Grotesk** with the balanced, highly readable neutrality of **Work Sans**. 

- **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Engineered with expressive brutalist energy, featuring idiosyncratic glyph cuts and tight tracking. Headlines set the tone of confident creative direction and command the primary visual weight within bento tiles.
- **Body & Longform (Work Sans):** Grounded and utilitarian. It neutralizes the eccentricities of Space Grotesk in case studies, project notes, and process descriptions, maintaining effortless legibility at dense information scales.
- **Labels & Metadata (Space Grotesk):** Rendered in mid-to-bold weights, frequently uppercase or lightly tracked, giving system metrics, tools tags, and date stamps an authoritative, technical artifact appearance.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture is driven by an asymmetric **Bento Grid layout** mapped to an uncompromising vertical and horizontal rhythm:

- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column desktop grid collapsing cleanly into 6 columns on tablet and 1 column on mobile. Bento cells use varying span ratios (e.g., 8+4, 4+4+4, 7+5, 12) to produce dynamic visual rhythm without breaking exterior edge alignments.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Component interiors rely on generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to counterbalance dense typography and hard outlines, preserving breathing room within tightly grouped modules.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Fluid container up to max 1280px, `margin-lg` outer gutter, `gutter-lg` module separation.
  - **Tablet (640px–1023px):** 6-column reflow, `margin-md`, `gutter` module separation.
  - **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column stacked stack, `margin` boundary margins, vertical sequence ordered strictly by visual priority.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons diffused, soft blur shadows in favor of **Neubrutalist hard-offset projection planes**:

- **Standard Elevation:** Bento modules feature a solid 1.5px border of `#E4E4E7` on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with no shadow under resting state, keeping the interface airy and clean.
- **Accentuated Bento Elevation:** Key focal cards (such as featured campaigns or profile blocks) feature a sharp, unblurred hard shadow: `4px 4px 0px #0F172A`.
- **Interactive Tactility (Buttons & Clickable Modules):**
  - **Resting:** `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #0F172A`.
  - **Hover:** The element translates `translate(-1px, -1px)` and the shadow expands to `4px 4px 0px #0F172A`.
  - **Active / Press:** The element snaps downward via `translate(3px, 3px)` with `box-shadow: 0px 0px 0px #0F172A`, delivering an immediate, satisfying mechanical click effect.
- **Badges & Inset Overlays:** Dark slate containers sit flush against surfaces or rely on 1px crisp separation strokes rather than gradients or atmospheric drops.

## Shapes

The design system employs a calibrated balance of softened brutality:

- **Bento Modules:** Use prominent 16px (`rounded-lg`) to 24px (`rounded-xl`) outer corner radiuses, eliminating harsh architectural points while reinforcing the friendly, contemporary creative persona.
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** Formed with consistent 10px to 12px corners, establishing unified physical geometry across smaller interactive footprints.
- **Status Pills & Micro Chips:** Utilize full capsule geometry (`rounded-full`) to contrast against the broad, squarish bento proportions.
- **Image Frames:** Mirror the exact interior radius of parent cards minus the internal offset padding, maintaining concentric harmonic curvature.

## Components

### Bento Cards
- **Base Card:** Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`), border of `1.5px solid #E4E4E7`, `rounded-xl` corners.
- **Marigold Highlight Card:** Solid `#F59E0B` fill, border of `2px solid #0F172A`, offset shadow `4px 4px 0px #0F172A`. Typography within defaults to `#0F172A`.
- **Dark Metric Module:** Solid `#0F172A` fill, borderless or `#1E293B` hairline, high-contrast off-white body copy with `#F59E0B` statistical accents.

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#0F172A` background, pure white Space Grotesk label, border `2px solid #0F172A`, hard shadow `3px 3px 0px #F59E0B`. Active state translates `+3px, +3px` to collapse the shadow.
- **Accent Action:** Solid `#F59E0B` fill, `#0F172A` label, border `2px solid #0F172A`, hard shadow `3px 3px 0px #0F172A`.
- **Secondary Ghost:** White background, `#0F172A` label, border `1.5px solid #0F172A`, hard shadow `2px 2px 0px #E4E4E7`.

### Badges & Chips
- **Category Badge:** Deep slate `#0F172A` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, label-caps typography, uppercase, `rounded-full` shape with `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- **Availability Indicator:** Pill-shaped badge featuring a pulsating vibrant emerald or marigold dot paired with slate uppercase copy on a pure white capsule outline.

### Input Fields
- Flat white surface, `2px solid #E4E4E7`, `rounded-md` radius.
- Focus state triggers border transition to `#0F172A` accompanied by an immediate `3px 3px 0px #0F172A` offset shadow. No soft blur rings.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Hard 2px solid `#0F172A` contours with stark squared corners for checkboxes (4px radius) and crisp circular forms for radios. Selected states snap to a solid `#F59E0B` fill with an internal slate checkmark.

### Media & Showcase Containers
- Internal media frames utilize a fine `1px solid #E4E4E7` frame with `overflow: hidden`. Media surfaces gently zoom on card hover while the parent frame maintains strict corner bounds.
